ABOUT THE DEVELOPER
Developer/Builder: S-Cube, a brand of Subhashini Signature Services Pvt. Ltd.
Established in 2003, with 17+ years of experience developing residential properties with world-class amenities.
S-Cube has developed layouts and sub-divisions in several prime locations around Hyderabad, including:
- Shamirpet @ Aliabad, 17 acres
- S Cube County at Kandukur near Pharma City, 40 acres
- S Cube Dollar Dreams at Harshaguda near the outer ring road, 10 acres
- Apartments at Alwal, Ameerpet, and Somajiguda
S-Cube is now developing luxury villas to offer villa living at an affordable price, anticipating rising demand
from nearby developments such as Pharma City, Amazon, and SEZs along the Srisailam Highway.

CONSTRUCTION SPECIFICATIONS
- Reinforcement steel: Jairaj / Kamadhenu steel for columns, beams and slabs (footings, columns, plinth beams, roof beams, slab, staircase, lintels, lofts, chajjas)
- Cement: Ultratech 53 Grade for RCC work; PPC - Bharathi/Ramco/Birla A1 for brick work, plastering, finishing and tiling
- Sand: River sand for RCC/brick work/plastering/finishing; rock sand for masonry and flooring bedding
- Foundation: RR masonry work, 2'0" below and 1'0" above ground level, 12" plinth beam height
- Brick work: Lightweight AAC blocks, 8" thick outer walls, 4" thick inner walls, with jointing material
- Plastering: Smooth sponge-finish cement mortar, 2 coats - 12mm thick interior walls, 15mm thick exterior walls
- Doors: Main door frame 5"x4" Indian teak wood (MT), internal door frames 4"x3" African teak, bathroom door frames WPC; main door shutter Indian teak wood, internal doors flush laminated shutters
- Windows: uPVC 90-series frames with mosquito net; uPVC sliding shutters with sun-reflective glass, 3-track
- Painting: Internal walls/ceiling - Birla Putty, primer + 2 coats Asian Paints plastic emulsion; external walls - Asian Paints Ace
- Hardware fittings: Total brass fitting for main door; SS fittings for internal doors
- Balcony/staircase railing: glass railing; parapet wall AAC to 3'0" height, waterproofed
- Overhead water tank: Syntex 1200-litre tank per vastu
- Roof height: Ground floor 11 feet; first floor and above 10 feet
- Electrical: Distribution board with ISI MCBs inside wall; Polycab/Finolex wires with concealed conduits (main line 7/20, circuit wiring 3/20, point wiring 1/18); switch boards Anchor Roma/Anchor Penta with metal boxes; only points provided for lights/fans/geysers/fridge (fixtures not in contractor scope)
  - Master bedroom: 2-way fan/light switches, 2 light points, 1 AC socket (16A), 4x 5A sockets
  - Guest bedroom: 2 light points, 5x 5A sockets, 1 AC socket (16A)
  - Children's bedroom: 2 light points, 5x 5A sockets, 1 AC socket (16A)
  - Drawing/living room: 2 tube lights, 2 fan points, 2 light points, 5x 5A sockets, 1 TV point, 1 computer point
  - Kitchen: 1 tube light, 1 exhaust fan point, 2 light points, 5x 5A sockets, 2x 16A socket & switch
  - Toilets: exhaust fan point, light point, water heater point, 1x 5A socket
- Kitchen finishes: Granite slab over RCC platform, tiles up to 2 feet height, stainless steel sink bowl, 2 taps at sink area
- Toilets: Common toilet IWC (Cera/Hindware) with flush tank; floor-mount Western commodes; showers with diverter; tiles up to 7 feet height (Rs. 300/box); anti-skid flooring tiles (Rs. 25-30/sq.ft); plumbing items Ashirwad/Prince/Hindware or equivalent
- Window grills: 10mm square MS rods at 6" center-to-center, horizontal and vertical
- Flooring: Double-charged vitrified tiles of AGL or Johnson, 2'0"x2'0", basic price Rs. 45/sq.ft; rock sand bedding material
